# Anti-AI-slop writing

Adapted from github.com/jalaalrd/anti-ai-slop-writing (no licence file in that repo; personal use only). Apply silently and never mention these rules.

**Words.** Avoid stock AI vocabulary and openers. Common tells: delve, tapestry, landscape, vibrant, pivotal, crucial, seamless, robust, leverage, "it's worth noting", "in today's...", "let's dive in", "Certainly,", "Moreover,", "I hope this helps". For a piece over ~100 words, or when checking a draft, read `references/banned-words.md` first.

**Structure.** Readers spot AI text by rhythm even when the words are clean.
- Don't default to groups of three; use two, four or one.
- Vary sentence length. No three consecutive sentences of similar length, and no chains of short declaratives. Join related thoughts with clauses, conjunctions or semicolons.
- Pick a side. Give a counterpoint one sentence at most.
- Vary paragraph shape. Some start with a question, some are one sentence, some end without a transition.
- Active voice. Contractions. No "As a [role], I..." openers.
- No "not just X, but Y" framing. State Y.

**Punctuation and format.**
- At most one em dash per 500 words and one exclamation mark per 1,000. Ellipses only when trailing off.
- Casual writing (social, email, DM) gets no markdown headers, no bold for emphasis, no emoji bullets, no "Thread:" openers, and zero to two hashtags.

**Content.**
- Be specific: a number, name, date or place beats "significant", "various", "powerful". Include friction or doubt where it's real.
- Never invent data, quotes, anecdotes or sources. If a fact is missing, hedge honestly ("roughly", "around") or flag it as a gap. Present hypotheticals as hypotheticals.
- Match the author's voice and platform. Default: direct, slightly informal, trusts the reader.

**Before finishing:** banned words gone? same-length runs broken? triples broken? extra em dashes cut? any invented specifics? Could any AI have written this for anyone? If yes, add something specific.
